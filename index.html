Most startups
get design wrong.

They burn runway "doing it themselves"

They wait too long and ship something embarrassing. Or patch it together themselves quickly and downplay the value of their product with bad design.

They hire freelancers and Jr. designers

What they actually need is a senior creative partner who thinks like a co-founder, works alongside their engineers, and stays long enough to make a real difference.

They think good design is a luxury

They imagine design is either not important or out of reach. So they make concessions and it ends up costing them time and money and hurting them in the long term.

I've worked with startups for 17+ years

I know makes good design great. This attention to detail helps me and my team build strong relationships with clients, as they know we will put the same care into their project that they would.
